# 3DFabrica - Beta Quickstart Guide

Welcome to the 3DFabrica Beta! This guide will help you get started with our digital fabric studio.

## 🔑 1. How to Login
1. Navigate to [3-d-fabrica.vercel.app](https://3-d-fabrica.vercel.app).
2. Click **"Sign In"** or **"Start Free Trial"** in the header.
3. You will be redirected to the Google OAuth login page.
4. Sign in with your Google account.
5. After successful login, you will be automatically redirected to the **Fabric Studio**.

## 🛋 2. How to Use the Studio
### Select a Model
- In the **Edit** tab on the sidebar, choose between the **Modern Sofa** or the **Curtain** model.
- The 3D view will update automatically.

### Apply Fabrics
- Browse our **Library** of premium upholstery and curtain fabrics.
- Click any fabric to apply it to your selected 3D model.
- You can also upload your own fabrics in the **Uploads** tab.

### Upload Your Own Fabric
1. Go to the **Uploads** tab.
2. Click **"Upload New Fabric"** and select an image from your computer (JPG/PNG).
3. We recommend images at least 512x512 pixels for best results.
4. Once uploaded, your fabric will appear in the custom list and can be applied instantly.

### Customize the Look
- Go to the **Edit** tab to fine-tune the visualization:
  - **Tiling Scale**: Control pattern repetition.
  - **Rotation**: Rotate the fabric pattern.
  - **Surface Roughness**: Make fabrics look more matte or shiny (Silk vs Cotton).

## 📄 3. How to Export PDF
1. Once you're happy with your design, click **"Export Technical Sheet"** at the bottom of the sidebar.
2. A new window will open with a professional layout showing your design and its technical parameters.
3. Use your browser’s print dialog to save it as a PDF or print it directly.

## 🖱 4. Navigation Controls
- **Rotate**: Left-click and drag on the 3D canvas.
- **Zoom**: Use your mouse wheel to scroll in and out.
- **Pan**: Right-click and drag to move the camera view.
- **Auto-Sidebar**: Move your mouse to the left edge (within 30px) to expand the sidebar. Move it away to collapse.
